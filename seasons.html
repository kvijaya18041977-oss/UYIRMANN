AGRICULTURAL RHYTHM

Three important crop seasons.

Tamil Nadu agriculture includes different cropping periods. The exact crop calendar can vary by region, water availability, soil and local farming practices.

01 • JUNE — SEPTEMBER

Kuruvai

A short-duration rice-growing season associated especially with areas that have suitable irrigation and water availability.

02 • SEPTEMBER — JANUARY

Samba

A major rice-growing season in Tamil Nadu, with cultivation timing influenced by rainfall, irrigation and regional conditions.

03 • WINTER PERIOD

Navarai

Another rice-growing period found in parts of Tamil Nadu, often associated with available irrigation after the main cropping seasons.

FROM SEED TO HARVEST

The rhythm of a crop.

A crop passes through several stages. Farmers observe the field continuously and adjust their decisions as conditions change.

01 • PREPARE

Prepare the Land

Soil preparation creates suitable conditions for planting and crop establishment.

02 • PLANT

Sow the Crop

Seeds or seedlings are introduced according to the crop and local cultivation method.

03 • GROW

Care for the Field

Water, nutrients, weeds, pests and crop health are monitored throughout the growing period.

04 • OBSERVE

Read the Conditions

Weather, soil moisture and crop development help guide farming decisions.

05 • HARVEST

Bring in the Crop

When the crop reaches the appropriate stage, harvesting begins and the next cycle approaches.
