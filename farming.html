Farming is more than growing a crop. It is a journey of soil, water, seeds, knowledge, patience and the people who work with the land.

THE JOURNEY

How a Crop Comes to Life

From preparing the land to bringing the harvest home, every stage of farming plays an important role.

01
🌱

Land Preparation

Farmers prepare the soil by ploughing, levelling and creating suitable conditions for the crop.

02
🌾

Seed Selection

Good-quality seeds are selected according to the crop, soil conditions, climate and farming season.

03
🌿

Sowing

Seeds are placed in the soil using suitable spacing and sowing methods for healthy growth.

04
💧

Irrigation

Water is supplied to crops according to their needs through rainfall, canals, wells and irrigation systems.

05
🪴

Crop Nutrition

Plants need essential nutrients from the soil. Farmers manage soil fertility to support healthy crops.

06
🛡️

Crop Protection

Farmers monitor crops for pests, diseases and weeds and use suitable management methods.

07
☀️

Growth & Care

Crops are regularly observed as farmers manage water, weeds, nutrients and changing weather conditions.

08
🌾

Harvest

When the crop reaches maturity, farmers harvest it and prepare it for storage, processing or markets.

01 • THE FOUNDATION

Healthy Soil.
Healthy Crops.

Soil is one of the most important resources in agriculture. Its structure, moisture, organic matter and nutrient status influence the growth of crops.

✓
Preparing the soil creates suitable conditions for roots and seeds.
✓
Organic matter can contribute to soil structure and support soil life.
✓
Water management helps crops receive the moisture they need.
STEP BY STEP

The Farmer's Calendar

Farming decisions change throughout the crop cycle. Farmers observe the land and crop and respond accordingly.

🌱 Prepare

Assess the land, prepare the soil and plan the crop according to local conditions.

🌾 Plant

Select suitable seeds and establish the crop using an appropriate sowing method.

💧 Grow

Manage irrigation, nutrients, weeds and crop protection throughout the growing period.

🔎 Observe

Regular observation helps farmers identify changes in plant health, pests and weather conditions.

🌾 Harvest

Once the crop reaches maturity, harvesting begins and the produce moves toward storage or markets.

FARMING VALUES

Agriculture Needs Balance

Sustainable agriculture considers the relationship between soil, water, crops, farmers and the environment.

🌍

Protect the Soil

Maintaining soil health supports productive farming over the long term.

💧

Use Water Wisely

Efficient water management is important, especially in areas where water is limited.

🌱

Grow Diversity

Crop diversity can help farming systems respond to changing environmental conditions.

🐝

Support Nature

Healthy agricultural landscapes depend on interactions between crops, soil organisms and wildlife.

📡

Use Technology

Sensors, satellite data and smart farming tools can help farmers make informed decisions.

👨‍🌾

Value Farmers

Farmers' knowledge, experience and decisions remain central to the agricultural system.
