Knowledge Is Part of Agriculture

A field can teach — but people can teach each other too.

Farming involves observation, experience, planning, adaptation and continuous learning.

Traditional agricultural knowledge can exist alongside modern science, digital tools and new research.

UYIRMANN brings these different worlds together to make agriculture easier to explore and understand.
