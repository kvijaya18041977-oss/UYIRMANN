Soil & Agriculture

Every Harvest
Begins With Soil.

Beneath every field is a living world of minerals, organic matter, water, roots and microorganisms. Explore the foundation that supports Tamil Nadu's agricultural landscapes.

Explore Soil Explore Crops
The ground beneath the harvest. Soil is a foundation for crops, ecosystems and food.
01 — The Living Ground

Soil is much more than dirt.

Soil provides physical support for plants and contains water, air, minerals and organic material that interact with roots and other living organisms.

Different landscapes have different soil characteristics. These differences can influence what grows well and how agricultural systems develop.

Healthy agriculture begins below the surface.
Understanding soil helps us understand crops, water, biodiversity and the wider farming ecosystem.
02 — Soil Landscapes

Different soils.
Different landscapes.

Tamil Nadu contains a variety of soil environments. Their properties can vary according to location, climate, parent material, drainage and landscape.

🟤
Common soil group

Red Soil

Red soils are found across parts of Tamil Nadu and are associated with many agricultural landscapes. Their characteristics vary with location and land conditions.

⬛
Important agricultural soil

Black Soil

Black soils are known for their clay-rich characteristics in many areas and can retain substantial amounts of water.

🌾
River landscapes

Alluvial Soil

Alluvial soils develop from sediments deposited by water. River and delta landscapes can contain agriculturally important alluvial environments.

🟡
Sandy environments

Sandy Soil

Sandy soils have larger particles and different drainage characteristics compared with finer-textured soils.

🪨
Landscape dependent

Lateritic Soil

Lateritic soils occur in certain landscapes where weathering and rainfall conditions have influenced soil formation.

🌱
Organic-rich environments

Coastal & Wetland Soils

Coastal and wetland landscapes can contain distinctive soil environments influenced by water, sediments and local ecology.

03 — A Hidden Ecosystem

A world beneath
our feet.

Soil contains a complex combination of physical materials, water, air, organic matter and living organisms.

🪱

Soil Organisms

Soil can support earthworms, insects and many microscopic organisms.

🌿

Roots

Roots anchor plants and interact directly with the soil environment.

💧

Water

Soil can store and transmit water through spaces between soil particles.

🍂

Organic Matter

Decomposed plant and animal material contributes to soil organic matter.

04 — Soil Life

The field's
hidden community.

A productive agricultural landscape is connected to the living processes happening inside the soil. Roots, microorganisms, organic matter, air and water all interact.

🌱
Plant Roots Roots interact with soil and help plants obtain water and nutrients.
🦠
Microorganisms Microscopic life participates in decomposition and nutrient cycling.
🍃
Organic Matter Plant residues and other organic materials contribute to the soil environment.
🪱
Soil Animals Larger soil organisms can influence the structure and biological activity of soil.
